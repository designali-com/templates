# Running a clinic's growth from one screen

**Predict Automation** is the operations side of PREDICT: a staff portal that helps a clinic turn marketing spend into booked assessments.

Behind a simple sign-in sits a role-based system, with a **no-password demo** using fictional data so anyone can explore it.

## Roles

- **Operations manager:** leads, call queue, WhatsApp, reports and ad ROI.
- **Telecaller:** their own leads and today's call queue, nothing more.
- **New staff:** a welcome link from the admin sets up their account.

## Why it exists

A clinic gets leads from ads, WhatsApp and referrals. Without a system, follow-ups slip and nobody knows which campaign works. The portal keeps each lead visible from first click to booked test.

## Design notes

- A single sign-in card and clear role selection, so the first minute is calm.
- Each role sees only what it needs. A telecaller never gets lost in reports.
- Dense tables stay readable with strong hierarchy and status colours.
- Built with Next.js and Tailwind, reusing [Predict UI](https://predict.designali.com) components.
