# The PREDICT website, built properly

**Predict Web** is my code-first build of the public PREDICT experience. It tells one story: *muscle is the next vital sign*, and blood can show structural ageing years before symptoms appear.

It sits beside [predict.fit](https://predict.fit) and the [Predict UI](https://predict.designali.com) design system, and reuses the same components.

## What it covers

- **Hero and the organ that predicts everything:** big, statement-led typography.
- **Why muscle, why blood:** four cards for cardiovascular, metabolism, bone & joint and cognitive health.
- **Bio-AgeClocks™:** MuscleAGE™, OrthoAGE™ and LegAGE™ explained with the Bio Clock component.
- **How it works:** Day 1 at-home sample, Days 2-3 your score, Days 4-7 specialist protocol.
- **Ecosystem:** VO2 max, advanced blood tests, HYROX training, customised supplements and a longevity retreat.
- **Ask anything:** an animated Q&A about muscle age and inflammation.
- **Testimonials, FAQ and waitlist.**
- **Biomarker pages:** Aldolase A, CRP, MDA and Vitamin D.
- **Ask AI buttons** that open ChatGPT, Claude or Grok with a prefilled question about PREDICT.

## Design and build notes

- Light and dark image variants, so the brand holds up in both themes.
- Marquee and testimonial rows that stay smooth on mobile.
- Biomarker content is structured data, so one source feeds pages, reports and AI prompts.
- Built with Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS and Base UI, deployed on Vercel.
