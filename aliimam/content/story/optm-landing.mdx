# One page, one action: book the assessment

The main [optm site](/story/optm) explains everything. This landing page does one job: take a visitor from an ad click to a booked assessment with as little friction as possible.

## Structure

1. **Hero:** *"Pain that keeps coming back ends here"*, with a muscle-age vs real-age hook.
2. **The missing insight:** why scans alone miss the root cause.
3. **MuscleAGE™:** one blood sample estimates your muscle age.
4. **Five systems, one score:** a quick visual of what is measured.
5. **Conditions:** knee, back, neck, shoulder, hip, sciatica and more.
6. **Patient stories:** pain score and MuscleAGE™ changes, with consent disclaimers.
7. **Book a test:** name, email, phone and "where is your pain?".

## Design decisions

- A single clear call to action, repeated at the right moments.
- Sticky phone and WhatsApp options, since most patients prefer to talk.
- Light pages that load fast on mid-range phones.
- Copy matched to the ad that sent the visitor.
