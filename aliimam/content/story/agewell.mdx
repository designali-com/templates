# Ageing well starts before it hurts

**Agewell India** is a healthy-ageing programme from [OPTM Healthcare](/story/optm). It helps adults notice changes in muscle strength, mobility and metabolic health early, before they limit daily life.

## Who it is for

- Adults over 50
- People losing strength or muscle
- Anyone with joint pain or recovering from illness
- Families checking on a parent

## Page structure

- **The problem:** early signs of decline, in plain words.
- **The approach:** clinical assessment, personalised recommendations and phytotherapy.
- **MuscleAGE™:** biological muscle age vs chronological age, with inflammation markers like IL-6 tracked over time.
- **Four-step protocol:** assessment, plan, monitoring, outcome reporting.
- **What we measure:** muscle, movement, bone & joint, metabolic, inflammation and functional ageing.
- **Six-step programme:** online assessment through to optimisation.
- **Team, FAQ and contact:** WhatsApp and phone, Mon-Sat 9am-7pm.

## Design approach

This audience needs **big type, high contrast and simple steps**. I used a warm, calm look, generous spacing and large tap targets, and kept clinical words behind plain explanations. A disclaimer notes that examples are illustrative.
