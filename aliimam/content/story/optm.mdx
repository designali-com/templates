# Pain that keeps coming back ends here

**optm** is a precision healthcare clinic in South Extension, New Delhi. It treats chronic pain (knee, back, neck, shoulder, hip and more) by finding its biological cause. Instead of relying only on X-rays and MRIs, optm measures **40+ blood biomarkers across five biological systems** and builds a personalised, evidence-based protocol around them.

I joined as **Head of Design in July 2026**. I own how optm looks, feels and communicates, across the website, patient reports, clinic materials and campaigns.

## Why this matters

Around **220 million Indians** live with chronic musculoskeletal pain, and fewer than 1 in 10 get a root-cause diagnosis. Most patients who reach optm have already been told their joints are "damaged" and that surgery is the next step. They arrive tired, sceptical and often scared.

That made the design brief very human:

- Earn trust from someone who has been let down before.
- Explain the science without sounding like a lab report.
- Make "biomarkers" feel like hope, not homework.
- Make booking a first assessment as easy as sending a WhatsApp message.

## What I worked on

### 01. Branding and identity

optm's positioning is sharp: **measure what imaging can't**. I built the whole brand around that idea.

- **Wordmark and logo system:** lowercase **optm**, with a compact mark for avatars, favicons and stamps.
- **Colour:** a restrained clinical palette with a calm green accent (also used in the MuscleAGE™ emblem) and red reserved for pain highlights.
- **Typography:** editorial headlines with a highly legible text face, so reports read as easily as ads.
- **Illustration and photography direction:** older adults moving freely (football in the park, laughing outdoors) paired with simple red-highlight body illustrations that show exactly where it hurts.
- **Tone of voice:** confident in the science, plain-spoken with patients. *"We don't start with treatment. We start with understanding."*
- **Brand guidelines:** a short, usable rulebook so clinic staff and freelancers stay on-brand.

### 02. The main website

[optmhealthcare.in](https://optmhealthcare.in) is designed as a guided argument, section by section:

1. **Hero:** *"Pain that keeps coming back ends here at optm"*, with Knee Age 58 vs Biological Age 47 as a visual hook.
2. **Why does pain return?** Name the frustration the patient already feels.
3. **MuscleAGE™:** one blood sample estimates your muscle age, using the Bio-Musculo Index™.
4. **Five systems, one score:** Inflammatory, Endocrine–Mineral, Bone & Cartilage, Muscle–ECM and Neurovascular.
5. **The optm Method:** Knee, Neck & Back, Muscle longevity, and Obesity & GLP-1 pathways.
6. **The Knee Protocol:** Day 1–3 assess, Day 7 treat, Day 30/60 measure, Day 90 reassess.
7. **Conditions we treat:** 20+ conditions, each with a custom illustration, in a scrolling marquee.
8. **Patient stories:** real MuscleAGE™ and pain-score changes, with a clear consent disclaimer.
9. **The science:** OrthoAGE™, photobiomodulation, early knee assessment and phytotherapy research.
10. **Clinical team and FAQ:** Dr. Apurba Ganguly and the 34+ years behind the protocols.
11. **Book an assessment:** one clear action, repeated at the right moments, with a short form (name, email, phone, where is your pain).

### 03. Landing pages and microsites

One site cannot serve every audience, so I built focused pages for specific journeys:

- **[OPTM Landing](/story/optm-landing):** a conversion-first page for ad campaigns.
- **[Agewell](/story/agewell):** a healthy-ageing programme for adults over 50 and their families.
- **[Dr. Apurba Ganguly](/story/apurba):** a credibility-led site for the founder.

All three share one component set and one visual language, so they feel like a family.

### 04. Social media

Social is where most patients first meet optm, so it got its own system:

- **Templates** for Instagram and Facebook posts, carousels and stories, built so the team can swap copy and ship the same day.
- **Educational carousels:** "Why your knee hurts when it rains", "CRP explained in 30 seconds", "What is sarcopenia?". Each turns one biomarker or condition into five simple slides.
- **Patient-story cards:** MuscleAGE™ before/after, pain score, programme length and key findings, always with the consent disclaimer.
- **Reels and short video:** motion graphics for the Knee Protocol timeline and biological-age reveals.
- **Condition explainers** for knee, back, neck, shoulder and sciatica, reusing the red-highlight illustration style.
- **WhatsApp creatives:** broadcast banners and booking prompts, since that is how most patients actually book.
- **Content calendar:** a repeating weekly rhythm of education, proof, myth-busting and a booking call to action.

### 05. Ads and campaigns

- Google and Meta ad creatives that lead with the pain point and point to the landing page.
- Seasonal and awareness campaigns (World Arthritis Day, Senior Citizens Day, winter joint-pain season).
- Creatives for newer programmes: muscle longevity and obesity / GLP-1 care.

### 06. Assessments and reports

optm uses the same science as Predict, including **MuscleAGE™** and **OrthoAGE™**. I designed the report dashboards that bring biological age, biomarker analysis (CTX-II, COMP, IL-6, hs-CRP, CK-MM, Aldolase A), inflammation profile, risk assessment and recommendations into one readable view.

Because I also work on [Predict](/story/predict), the two products share visual DNA and many components from the Predict UI system.

### 07. Print and in-clinic materials

- Clinic signage for South Extension I, brochures and appointment cards.
- Patient handouts for exercises (Movement RX) and home care.
- Prescription pads, report covers and visiting cards.
- Standees and posters that explain the five systems at a glance.

### 08. Science, made credible

optm's foundation is 34+ years of research and 120+ published studies. The "Science" section presents publications with Wiley, SAGE, Elsevier and PubMed/NIH references so they feel substantial but approachable: proof, not a wall of citations.

## Leading design

As Head of Design my focus is as much on **consistency** as on craft:

- One design language across web, reports, print and social.
- Reusable templates so the team can produce on-brand material quickly.
- Close work with clinicians to make sure every claim and visual is accurate.
- Patient-first copy: short sentences, real words, no jargon without explanation.
