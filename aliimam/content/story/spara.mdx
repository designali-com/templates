# A place to return to

**Spara Resort** is a boutique nature and wellness retreat at Pushpanjali Farms on the Dwarka Expressway, New Delhi. It is made for unhurried stays: weekend getaways, family celebrations, weddings and longer breaks.

The brief was the opposite of most hospitality sites: **less noise, more calm**.

## What I designed and built

- **Hero:** a quiet introduction with two actions, *View gallery* and *Book a call*.
- **Dining:** a cafe and restaurant for relaxed meals and evenings.
- **Rooms:** calm interiors shown with space around them.
- **Boutique concept:** the philosophy of slow days and quiet spaces.
- **Location:** clear travel times (India Gate 40 min, Ambience Mall 10 min, Yashobhoomi 5 min, IGI Airport 15 min).
- **Experience:** poolside and "Unwind" moments, plus weddings, social and corporate events.
- **Contact:** phone, email, address and map, with online booking through an external system.
- **Longevity Protocols:** a coming-soon section that links the resort to wellness.

## Design approach

Soft natural palette, elegant type, big photography and slow, subtle motion. Nothing flashy, so the place itself is the star. The site is fast and works well on phones, where most bookings start.
