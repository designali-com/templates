# A portfolio is a product, too

Most portfolios are galleries. This one is designed like a product, for one user: **a recruiter or hiring manager with a few minutes and a role to fill.**

## The problem

My work spans a lot: design leadership, product design, design systems, brand, motion and frontend code. Shown all at once, that reads as noise. A hiring team needs to answer three questions fast:

1. **What does he do?** A clear title and one-line value, above the fold.
2. **Can he do it for us?** Proof mapped to the role they're hiring for.
3. **How do we talk to him?** Email, CV, LinkedIn and a calendar, never more than one click away.

## Structure

- **Hero:** title, availability, location and three actions (case studies, CV, email).
- **Numbers:** years of experience, component views, monthly Pinterest views and live products, each with a source.
- **Case studies:** every card leads with my role, company and year, then the outcomes.
- **What I bring to a team:** one card per role (Head of Design, Founding Designer, Design Engineer, Product, Frontend, Brand), each linked to proof.
- **Experience, process and toolkit:** short, scannable, and backed by the full story on the About page.
- **FAQ for recruiters:** roles I'm open to, location, remote work and how I work with teams.
- **Contact:** email, LinkedIn, CV and a booking calendar.

## Design decisions

- **One source of truth.** All content lives in one typed data file, so the home page, about page, case studies and footer never disagree.
- **Case studies in MDX.** Each project is a Markdown file with a structured summary panel (role, company, year, scope, live link, outcomes) on top.
- **Keep the brand.** The dotted grid, serif headlines and blue accent come from the designali system, so the portfolio feels like my work, not a template.
- **Search and AI friendly.** Clear metadata and Person structured data, plus "Ask AI about Ali Imam" links in the footer.

## Built with

Next.js, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Base UI, Fumadocs MDX and Motion, deployed on Vercel.
