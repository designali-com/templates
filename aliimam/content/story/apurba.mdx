# Putting the science behind a face

Patients trust people before they trust protocols. This site presents **Dr. Apurba Ganguly**, founder and Chief Scientific Officer of [OPTM Healthcare](/story/optm), and the research behind every optm treatment.

## Sections

- **Hero:** a clear introduction with an assessment request form and WhatsApp contact.
- **Experience and credentials:** 34+ years of research, published work and awards.
- **Patient stories:** knee pain and osteoarthritis cases with before/after pain scores.
- **The OPTM approach:** conventional care vs a biology-focused, personalised, long-term model.
- **Conditions treated:** joint, back, neck and shoulder pain, sciatica, arthritis, sports injuries and nerve pain.
- **The science:** OrthoAGE™, MuscleAGE™, photobiomodulation, gait screening and topical therapy papers.
- **Care team:** doctors, physiotherapist and nutritionists, with video consults and follow-up.
- **FAQ and contact:** address, phone, WhatsApp, email and clinic hours.

## Design notes

- Editorial layout with large portrait and quote, so it reads as a person's story, not a brochure.
- Publications shown as clean cards with source logos, not long citation lists.
- Patient results always carry a consent and "results vary" note.
