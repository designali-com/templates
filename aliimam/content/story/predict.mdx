# Making health visible before it's felt

PREDICT is a muscle-health and biological-age platform from Predict Fit (Control Age Health Pvt. Ltd.). The premise is bold and simple: **muscle is the next vital sign**. A blood panel and body composition scan can show structural ageing years before you feel it, and PREDICT turns that into something a person can actually understand and act on.

I joined as **Design Engineer in July 2026**. My job sits right in the middle of the product: shaping how PREDICT looks, how it speaks, and how it is built.

> **Health should be measured before it is felt.**

## The problem

Health data is dense. A single PREDICT assessment produces a 45-biomarker blood panel, body composition results, and biological age scores across six physiological systems. To a clinician that is rich information. To most people it is a spreadsheet of unfamiliar words like *Aldolase A*, *hs-CRP* and *MDA*.

The design challenge was never "make it pretty". It was:

- How do you make someone *care* about a number like MuscleAGE™?
- How do you show a result is outside range without causing panic?
- How do you keep a premium, invitation-only brand feeling calm and scientific, not clinical and cold?
- How do you let a small team ship dashboards, reports and marketing pages that all feel like one product?

## What I worked on

### 01. Branding and identity

PREDICT had to feel scientific and premium without feeling cold.

- **Logo and wordmark** with a clear, confident treatment that works from favicon to billboard.
- **Bio-AgeClocks™ naming system:** MuscleAGE™, OrthoAGE™ and LegAGE™ share one visual grammar, so each new clock feels familiar.
- **Colour and typography** documented in [Predict UI](https://predict.designali.com) so the brand lives in code, not just a PDF.
- **Mascot and Loader:** small moments of personality that make waiting for results feel friendly.
- **Tone of voice:** short, provocative lines like *"There is an organ that predicts everything."* and *"The earlier you know, the more you can change."*
- **Brand guidelines** for partners, clinics and the press.

### 02. Launch film and motion

PREDICT launched as an invitation-only experience for founding members. I worked on the launch film and the motion language around it: biological-age reveals, animated dials, and the "MuscleAge™ now 2.4 years younger" moment.

### 03. The website

[predict.fit](https://predict.fit) had to do a lot of explaining in very little time. The page walks a visitor from one provocative idea (muscle predicts everything) to a clear journey:

1. **The organ:** muscle, and why its decline drags four systems with it.
2. **The signal:** blood as a live data stream that carries your true age.
3. **Why muscle, why blood:** cardiovascular, metabolism, bone & joint, cognitive health.
4. **Bio-AgeClocks™:** 30,000+ clinical data points, patent filed, peer-reviewed validation.
5. **Inside the platform:** one dashboard for blood biomarkers and muscle imaging.
6. **How it works:** Day 1 at home, Days 2-3 your score, Days 4-7 specialist review.
7. **The ecosystem:** VO2 max, advanced blood tests, HYROX training, custom supplements and a longevity retreat.
8. **Ask anything:** an AI Q&A that makes biological ageing *audible*.
9. **Testimonials, FAQ and waitlist.**

Supporting pages like the **biomarker library** (Aldolase A, CRP, MDA, Vitamin D) follow the same calm, editorial rhythm.

### 04. Social media and content

- **Launch teasers** built around the single line *"There is an organ that predicts everything."*
- **Educational carousels:** what is muscle age, what is hs-CRP, why muscle is the next vital sign.
- **Biomarker explainers** that reuse the Reference Range component as a graphic.
- **Testimonial and founder-quote cards** (doctors, investors, longevity creators).
- **Short-form video:** the Bio Clock animating from biological to chronological age.
- **LinkedIn thought-leadership layouts** for the founder and scientific team.
- **Waitlist and invite creatives** that make the founding-member offer feel exclusive.

### 05. Bio-AgeClocks™ as a visual system

The heart of PREDICT is its age clocks. Each compares biological age with chronological age. I designed them as reusable visual pieces rather than one-off graphics:

- **Bio Clock:** the signature dial for biological vs. chronological age.
- **Age Bar:** a linear version for dense reports and comparisons over time.

Seeing *"your MuscleAGE™ improved by 2.4 years"* only works if the visual makes the change obvious in a second. Most of the iteration went into that.

### 06. Product, reports and operations

PREDICT is more than a marketing site. I also designed and built the product surfaces around it:

- **Results dashboard and reports** for blood biomarkers, body composition and age scores.
- **[Predict Web](/story/predict-web):** the public site, rebuilt as a fast, themeable Next.js app.
- **[Predict Clinic](/story/predict-clinic):** role-based clinic software for admins, front desk, doctors and diagnostics.
- **[Predict Automation](/story/predict-automation):** an operations portal for leads, calls, WhatsApp and ad ROI.

## Predict UI: a design system in code

The biggest piece of work is **[Predict UI](https://predict.designali.com)**, the design system behind PREDICT's dashboards, reports and clinical tools.

> **Components for clear health interfaces.**

Instead of a static Figma kit, I built it as a live, code-first system with documentation, so design and engineering share one source of truth.

### Foundations

Brand guidelines, colour, and typography, documented once and used everywhere.

### Components

22+ components built on **Base UI**: Accordion, Button, Calendar, Card, Dialog, Input, Popover, Progress, Radio Group, Sheet, Sidebar, Slider, Switch, Tabs, Toggle, Tooltip and more. Each one has its own docs page with live examples.

### PREDICT UI

Product-specific pieces that present a person's results: **Bio Clock, Age Bar, Mascot, Loader and Logo**.

### Health tools

This is the part I'm proudest of, because it is where design meets real health logic:

- **Body Measurements:** BMI and body surface area, with several clinical formulas.
- **Reference Range:** shows a biomarker value against its range, with a Z-score and a high/low flag (for example, hs-CRP).
- **Age Calculator:** exact age on a given date, including correction for prematurity.

### Health content

Biomarkers, indices and reference ranges, structured as content so the same data can power the product, the reports and the website.

## How I approached it

- **Calm over alarming.** Out-of-range values are clear, but colour, language and layout never shout.
- **One number first.** Every screen leads with the single result that matters, and detail comes after.
- **Built, not handed off.** I take components from Figma into React, Next.js, TypeScript and Tailwind myself, so what ships matches the design.
- **Systems over screens.** Every new screen should make the next one faster to build.

## Tools
