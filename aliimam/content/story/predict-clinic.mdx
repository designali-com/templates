# Software for the whole clinic visit

**Predict Clinic** is a clinic platform built around one promise: *start moving better in 7 days.* It covers the full patient journey, from front desk to doctor to diagnosis.

Sign-in offers a no-login demo with fictional data, plus standard login, email and OTP options.

## Roles

- **Clinic admin:** patients, courses, billing, stock and reports.
- **Front desk:** daily schedule, arrivals and payments.
- **Doctor:** consultations, prescriptions and exercise plans.
- **Predict (diagnosis):** blood tests, biomarkers and health reports.

## Design challenges

- Four very different users in one product, each needing a focused home screen.
- Doctors work fast, so prescriptions and exercise plans take minimum clicks.
- Biomarker results use the Reference Range pattern from [Predict UI](https://predict.designali.com): clear flags, no panic.
- OTP and passwordless login keep access easy for non-technical staff.

## Build

Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS and Base UI, with the shared health tools (Reference Range, Body Measurements, Age Calculator).
